Blockchain-Based Healthcare Record Management System

An institutional framework for the secure storage, cryptographic encryption, and consent-driven distribution of electronic medical records using distributed ledger technology.

Core Technical Infrastructure

The SafeHealth platform is built upon three foundational pillars of modern cryptographic science to ensure data immutability and privacy.

Cryptographic Encryption

All sensitive health data undergoes a strict double-encryption protocol. Data is first secured via symmetric AES-256 standards, followed by asymmetric Paillier homomorphic encryption.

Distributed Storage

Rather than relying on centralized institutional databases, encrypted payloads are distributed across the InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) to prevent unauthorized mass breaches.

Immutable Audit Log

Every transaction—including record access, consent delegation, and clinical prescription—is permanently recorded on an Ethereum-compatible distributed ledger.

System Methodology & Workflow

A standardized procedure for managing patient records between clinical entities.

  • I.
    Data Ingestion & Encryption

    The patient submits their medical file through the secure portal. The system instantly encrypts the document client-side and transmits the cipher to the decentralized storage network, returning a unique content identifier (CID).

  • II.
    Ledger Registration

    The acquired CID, alongside the patient's anonymized identifier, is registered via a smart contract on the blockchain. This transaction serves as an immutable timestamp of the record's creation.

  • III.
    Consent-Based Authorization

    The patient explicitly delegates decryption rights to registered medical institutions. This consent is logged on the blockchain, granting the institution temporary, auditable access to the requested file.
